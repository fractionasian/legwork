**Addendum (decided during build):** the end-of-route total pill ("18.3k")
was removed entirely — its anchor coordinate is a waypoint in every mode
(wp1 for loop/out-and-back, last wp for one-way), so it always rendered

delete (chosen — toolbar pill carries the exact total), back-off along route
(zoom-fragile), float beside waypoint (crowds the start marker's touch halo).

## Agreed changes (build queue)

### 1. Elevation chart — linear x-axis with pill-matched ticks
Chart x-axis shows non-round, unevenly spaced ticks (0.0, 1.6, 3.7, 5.7 …)
while map pills say 2k/4k/6k. Cause: per-point `toFixed(1)` labels on a
category axis with `maxTicksLimit: 10` (app.js:1303, 1336) — tick selection is
every-Nth-point, blind to distance. Fix: linear x-scale, ticks at round km at
the **same interval the km pills use, from a shared function**. Also fixes
mobile tick crowding.

### 2. Elevation chart — absolute elevation + fill artefact
Y-axis plots baseline-subtracted "Climb (m)", going all-negative on routes
starting at their high point. Side effect: with negative values Chart.js
`fill: true` fills from line *up* to y=0 (chart top), so per-segment gradeFill
paints full-height colour columns — the faint vertical stripes. Fix: plot
absolute elevation, label "Elevation (m)". Ascent/descent stats
(computeAscent, app.js:1285) unaffected. Commit together with #1.

### 3. km-pill intervals — runner-tier bands
Replace the bands in `updateDistanceMarkers` (app.js:1113–1120). Rationale:
match the split a runner at that distance thinks in, not just map density.

| Route | Interval | Pills | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 7 km | 1 km | ≤ 6 | couch-to-5k |
| 7–15 km | 2 km | 3–7 | confident 10–12k |
| 15–50 km | 5 km | 3–9 | half (4) / marathon (8) |
| > 50 km | 10 km | 5+ | ultra/cycling backstop — keep, don't delete |

Miles mirror: ≤4mi→1, 4–10→2, 10–35→5, >35→10. Marathon stays at 5 km (timing
mats / 5k-split mental model); 10 km tier starts >50 km, not >42.2. End pill
(exact total, 1dp) is unchanged and is why coarse intervals lose nothing.
Bonus: fewer pills ≈ fewer collisions where out/return legs share a corridor.

### 4. Desktop top-bar search (≥768px)
Address input lives only inside the side menu; the most common first action on
desktop (type a suburb) takes hamburger → search. Top bar has the space —
surface the input there on wide screens. Mobile keeps menu-only.

### 5. Saved-routes empty state
"SAVED ROUTES" header renders with nothing beneath it before any save — reads
broken. Add one muted line: "Routes you save appear here." (Header stays
visible per perceived-stability.)

### 6. Welcome modal trim
Six instructions → two (tap to add; drag the green circles to insert) + Start.
Everything else is discoverable or lives in Tips & shortcuts / the legend.
**Wizard explicitly rejected** — it's a mode, Legwork has no setup choices to
justify one; teach at moment of relevance or not at all.

### 7. Elevation scrub (steal from WristTopo, improved)
Pointer over the elevation chart → dot glides along the route polyline at the
corresponding position. Mapping is chart-x → cumulative distance → route point
(data already exists; binary search). Decisions:
- **Desktop: pure hover**, no click, no mode. Dot vanishes on pointer-leave.
- **Mobile: horizontal drag scrubs, vertical scrolls** (`touch-action: pan-y`
  on the canvas). Chosen over press-and-hold (undiscoverable) and
  desktop-only (half-present reads broken).
- **No auto-pan** — let the dot leave the viewport.
- **Tooltip yes** — "7.3 km · 24 m" at the scrub position.
- Dot is a third visual species: plain small dot, Flexoki accent, no number —
  must not look like a waypoint (blue numbered circle) or km pill (white pill).
- v2 only if asked: reverse mapping (hover route → highlight chart position).

## Decisions — no change

- **Midpoint insert stays a dashed circle.** It's the Google
  Maps/Komoot/Garmin convention (consistency-with-expectations), and the
  implementation already has the fat 44px hit target + waypoints-stack-above
  overlap rule. Ghost-dot-on-line-hover (Strava/Felt) is the only nicer
  pattern and is hover-only → desktop-only addition, low marginal value —
  revisit only if desktop users ask. Optional pure-CSS garnish (not queued):
  grow-on-hover 18→22px, `+` glyph on hover.
- **No zoom-gating/hover-gating of midpoint dots** — they ARE the
  discoverability of the least-discoverable feature; don't hide them for
  tidiness.
- **Straight-line routing profile (WristTopo has one): rejected** — a
  hiking/ski feature, pointless for a follow-real-paths running planner.
- **Pace/time estimates remain out of scope** (existing decision, reconfirmed
  seeing WristTopo's 3:27 estimate).
